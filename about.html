Our Foundation

The Four Tenets of Prime & Cut

Every animal sourced and portion trimmed across our counters adheres to non-negotiable craftsmanship standards.

100% Pasture Reared

Livestock forage freely on pesticide-free clover pastures, developing natural intramuscual marbling, rich omega-3 fatty acids, and clean flavor.

Himalayan Salt Dry Aging

Whole loins rest between 28 and 45 days in climate-controlled pink salt lockers, allowing natural enzymes to tenderize the muscle and intensify hazelnut aromatics.

Zero Chemical Interventions

Strict absence of sub-therapeutic antibiotics, growth hormones, nitrates, water injections, or artificial gas flushes. Pure meat as nature intended.

Nose-to-Tail Respect

Every primal cut, shank, marrow bone, and tallow trim is utilized. We honor the animal by leaving zero food waste behind on our chopping boards.

From Pasture to Counter

Transparent Sourcing. Know Your Farmer.

In an era where supermarket meats travel thousands of miles through anonymous factory feedlots, Prime & Cut champions direct regional farming. We shake hands with our ranchers in Vermont, Pennsylvania, and Virginia.

Every carcass delivered to our cold room comes tagged with its individual farm origin, feed history, processing date, and verification QR codes accessible right on your cut ticket.

Heritage Journey

Four Decades of Butchery Excellence

1984

The Humble Butcher Stall

Founded with a single maple butcher block, two cleavers, and local pasture beef sourced from neighboring valleys.

1998

Himalayan Salt Chamber Installed

Pioneered custom humidity-controlled dry aging lockers lined with authentic Pakistani pink salt bricks.

2012

Smokehouse Pit Expansion

Introduced traditional hardwood pitmaster smokers using aged hickory, post oak, and wild cherry wood chips.

2024 • Today

Active Cold-Chain Home Delivery

Launched refrigerated mobile fleet ensuring temperature-monitored cuts directly to front doors across the city.

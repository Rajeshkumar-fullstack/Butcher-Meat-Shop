Old-World Standards

Why Discerning Chefs Choose Us

We reject factory meat and mass processing. Our butcher counter operates on traditional butchery values where quality precedes speed.

Himalayan Salt Dry Aging

Our custom aging room utilizes genuine pink salt brick walls to regulate microclimate humidity, concentrating natural flavors for unmatched richness.

Bespoke Artisan Cutting

Tell our cutters your recipe. Whether you require paper-thin carpaccio, 2-inch cowboy ribeyes, or French-trimmed racks, we cut to your exact millimetre.

Ethical Farm Provenance

100% antibiotic-free and hormone-free animals raised on natural pastures with rotational grazing. Traceable from farm pasture to your dining table.

Chilled Vacuum Packing

Immediately vacuum-sealed in thick, medical-grade BPA-free barriers to prevent oxygenation and lock in natural juices until ready to cook.

Veterinary Inspected

Daily morning carcass intake certified to Grade A standards.

Integrity First

Our Strict Freshness & Sourcing Charter

We believe you deserve full transparency regarding what you feed your family. Every cut in our showcases adheres to our 4-point sourcing protocol:

Zero Added Hormones or Prophylactic Antibiotics

Naturally matured livestock raised without artificial growth promoters.

Unbroken 0°C – 4°C Temperature Chain

Monitored continuously with digital IoT temperature loggers at every stage.

Humanely Raised On Regenerative Pastures

Supporting small regenerative livestock farmers who respect the land.

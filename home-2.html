Master Your Cook

The Butcher’s Internal Temp Guide

Never guess internal temperature again. Pull your steaks 5°F before target to allow residual carryover cooking during resting.

Level 1 120°F – 125°F

Rare

Cool to warm red center with soft, yielding texture. Fat hasn't fully rendered.

Best Cuts: Center Filet Mignon, Lean Flank
Butcher's Choice
Level 2 130°F – 135°F

Medium-Rare

Warm red-pink center with buttery intramuscular fat completely rendered into the grain.

Best Cuts: Dry-Aged Ribeye, A5 Wagyu, NY Strip
Level 3 140°F – 145°F

Medium

Warm pink center with firm edges. Deep caramelized surface sear with moderate juiciness.

Best Cuts: Porterhouse, Lamb Loin Chops
Pitmaster Smoke 200°F – 205°F

Low & Slow Bark

Tough connective tissue and collagen melt into gelatinous moisture with crunchy dark bark.

Best Cuts: Texas Brisket, Pork Belly, Spare Ribs

The King of the Short Loin

The Porterhouse is a magnificent dual-muscle steak carved from the posterior end of the short loin. It features a generous center T-bone anchoring two distinct culinary treasures: Filet Mignon and New York Strip.

  • Primary Muscle: Precision trimmed along natural fat seams to eliminate tough gristle.
  • Flavor Profile: Deep savory richness developed through artisanal aging and ethical farming.

During aging in our salt lockers, moisture evaporates gradually, concentrating pure essence while natural enzymes soften every fiber.

Master Butcher's Reverse Sear Method

Thick-cut dry-aged steaks require gentle thermal progression to prevent overcooking the tenderloin before the strip forms its caramelized crust.

01

Dry Brine & Temper

Salt liberally with flaky sea salt 2 hours prior to cooking. Let rest on a wire rack at room temperature for 45 minutes to temper the core.

02

Low Oven / Smoker

Roast at 225°F (107°C) until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the strip reads 115°F (46°C) for medium-rare.

03

Cast Iron Flash Sear

Sear in screaming hot cast iron with beef tallow or ghee for 60 seconds per side. Baste with rosemary and garlic butter, then rest 8 minutes.

Storage Guidelines & Cold-Chain Integrity

  • Active Cold Range: Store in the coldest section of your home refrigerator at 32°F–36°F (0°C–2°C).
  • Shelf Life in Butcher Paper: 3 to 4 days from receiving your package.
  • Cryovac Packaging: If ordered vacuum-sealed, keep refrigerated up to 14 days or freeze immediately at -18°C for up to 6 months without freezer burn.

Our Capabilities

Eight Specialized Counter & Kitchen Services

From residential custom cut sheets to Michelin-starred restaurant portion control.

Service 01

Whole Carcass Portioning Service

Breakdown of whole and quarter beef, lamb, and heritage pork sides. Tailored cutting sheets allocating steaks, braises, and grind piles.

Service 02

Seam Trimming & Bone Frenching

Bone polishing for tomahawks and lamb racks, chining rib backbones, and hand-tying rolled roasts with linen twine for uniform cooking.

Service 03

Custom Fat-Ratio Artisan Grinds

Coarse single and double grinds blended to your ratio (80/20, 75/25, or 70/30). Ground fresh from whole brisket, short rib, and chuck.

Service 04

House-Crafted Rubs & Marinades

Coarse peppercorn crusts, rosemary-garlic tallow coatings, and Texas hardwood BBQ rubs massaged directly into your selected cuts.

Service 05

Hospitality & Chef Supply

Wholesale contracts for bistros, steakhouses, and private yacht chefs. Guaranteed calibrated weight consistency and scheduled morning cold delivery.

Service 06

Cryovac Chamber Vacuum Packing

Oxygen-free barrier seal safeguarding moisture, preventing freezer crystallization, and facilitating sous-vide temperature cooking.

Service 07

Whole-Animal Event Roasts

Suckling pigs, milk-fed lambs, and spatchcock organic turkeys trussed, seasoned, and delivered spit-ready for catering events and weddings.

Service 08

Waterstone Knife Care & Workshops

Waterstone knife sharpening for home cooks, alongside weekend evening butchery workshops covering pig breaking, tying, and sausage stuffing.

The Butcher's Protocol

How Our Custom Cutting Works

Four disciplined steps turning ethical pasture carcasses into table-ready culinary centerpieces.

01

Cut Consultation

Specify animal species, desired steak thickness (1.5" to 3"), bone preference, and intended cooking style (reverse sear, smoker, braise).

02

Cold Locker Selection

Our butcher selects the optimal aged loin or whole primal from our 0°C–2°C Himalayan salt room matching your target marbling.

03

Precision Hand Cutting

Hand-trimmed with Japanese boning and cimeter knives, weighed to the exact ounce, tagged, and custom-stamped on our physical butcher ticket.

04

Chilled Dispatch

Wrapped in traditional peach butcher paper or vacuum sealed, placed inside insulated cold-chain cartons, and dispatched same-day.
